# `Technion-Atlas-Lab` is the Lab's official GitHub organization

An older organization, `ATLAS-AI-LAB` (created June 2025, one forked repo, owner unknown), also claims to be the Lab. We made `Technion-Atlas-Lab` the official organization and leave `ATLAS-AI-LAB` untouched, because the new organization already requires two-factor authentication and has known owners who can hand it over when the Maintainer changes. Do not move the website or other Lab repos into `ATLAS-AI-LAB`.
