# ATLAS Lab website

The public website of ATLAS Lab at the Technion: who the Lab is, what it researches, and what it publishes.

## Language

### The Lab

**ATLAS Lab**:
Prof. Avi Mendelson's research group in the Technion's Faculty of Computer Science, focused on AI agent cybersecurity. Full name: ATLAS Research Laboratory, where ATLAS stands for AI Trust, Learning, Architecture, and Security.

**PI**:
The head of ATLAS Lab, Prof. Avi Mendelson.

**Research Area**:
A long-running research theme of the Lab, such as LLM safety and security. Every Publication and every Student Project belongs to exactly one Research Area.

**Publication**:
A paper by one or more Members, published in a journal or at a conference, or posted as a **Preprint** before publication.

**Figure**:
The one picture a Publication may show on its card, with alt text saying what it shows. Like a Member's photo, it is published resized and stripped of embedded metadata.

**Featured Publication**:
A Publication the Maintainer marked for Home to highlight.

**Student Project**:
A student project supervised by the Lab, either **Offered** (open for new students to take) or **Completed**.

**News item**:
A dated announcement on the News page, such as a talk, a visit or an accepted Publication.

**Lab mailbox**:
The Lab's shared email account, held by the PI and the Maintainer and passed on to each successor.

### People

**Member**:
A person currently in the Lab: the PI, research staff, or a current graduate student. Each holds one role: Lab Director (the PI), Postdoctoral Researcher, PhD Student, MSc Student or Research Staff.

**Role**:
A Member's position in the Lab. The People page shows one **role group** per role, in a fixed order; an Alumnus keeps the role they held as their **former role**.

**Initials avatar**:
The picture shown for a Member or Alumnus with no photo: the first letters of their first and last names.

**Alumnus** (plural **Alumni**):
A former Member.

**Collaborator**:
A person outside the Lab who works with it; credited on that work, never listed as a Member.

**Profile**:
The public information about one Member or Alumnus. Beyond name and role, each field appears only if that person gave Consent for it.

**Consent**:
A person's agreement to publish specific Profile fields, obtained by the Maintainer in any form before the data enters the repository.

### Running the site

**Maintainer**:
The Member responsible for the website.

**Site settings**:
The Lab-wide facts the whole site uses (Lab name, full name, mission, Lab mailbox, the site's address), kept in one file.

**Test content set**:
Fixed content, kept apart from the real content, that the slice tests build the site from, so real content can change without breaking them.

**Slice**:
One page or feature of the site built end to end in a single ticket: its content, templates, styles and tests.

**Visitor**:
A person browsing the site. In exploratory QA, also each of the two agents that plays one: the happy-path visitor and the adversarial visitor.

### Lifecycle

**Design phase**:
The period when the site shows only placeholder content; it ends with the PI's **Design approval**.

**Content phase**:
The period when real content is added; it ends with the PI's **Content approval**.

**Go-live**:
The moment the site starts being served at its Technion subdomain.
