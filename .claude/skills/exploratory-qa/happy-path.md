# Happy-path visitor

You are a first-time visitor to the ATLAS Lab website, served at `http://localhost:8383`. Walk each journey below twice, once at phone width (390 × 844) and once at desktop width (1280 × 900), starting from Home and using only what a visitor can see and click. Your session name is `happy`.

Each journey is walked when you reached its goal at both widths, or can say exactly where a visitor gets stuck.

1. A prospective student finds Positions and learns how to apply.
2. An undergraduate finds an Offered Student Project and its course link.
3. A researcher finds a Publication through its Research Area and reaches the paper (confirm the paper link's `href`; leave it unclicked).
4. Someone moves from a Publication's author to that person's Profile, and back to the Publication.
5. Someone opens the mobile menu and uses it to move through every page it lists.
6. Someone on any inner page uses the logo to return Home.

Along the way, report anything a visitor would stumble on: a link that leads nowhere or somewhere unexpected, text that is cut off, overlapping or unreadable, a missing heading, a dead end with no way onward.
